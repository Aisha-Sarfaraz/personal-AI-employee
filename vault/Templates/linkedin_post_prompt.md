# LinkedIn Post Prompt Template

Use this template to generate a professional LinkedIn post about business activity.

## Instructions

Write a LinkedIn post that:
- Highlights a recent business achievement, insight, or lesson learned
- Is between 150-300 words
- Uses a hook in the first line to grab attention
- Includes 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end
- Ends with a call to action or question to drive engagement
- Must include #AIAssisted to disclose AI involvement

## Context to Use

- Business goals from Business_Goals.md
- Recent activity from the last 7 days of audit logs
- Current projects and milestones

## Tone

Professional yet approachable. Share genuine insights. Avoid buzzwords.

## Example Structure

[Hook — attention-grabbing first line]

[Main insight or story — 2-3 paragraphs]

[Takeaway or lesson]

[Question or call to action]
