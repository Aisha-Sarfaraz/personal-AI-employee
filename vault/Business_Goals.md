# 🎯 Business Goals

## 🔑 Mission

> [!note] Our Purpose
> Build a Personal AI Employee (FTE) system that autonomously handles routine business operations while maintaining human oversight for sensitive actions.

## 📊 Current Priorities

1. **Bronze Tier Completion** — Establish core infrastructure with filesystem watcher, reasoning pipeline, and HITL approval workflow
2. **System Reliability** — Ensure all pipeline actions are audited and recoverable
3. **Human Trust** — Demonstrate safe operation through transparent logging and approval gates

## 📏 Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Pipeline end-to-end time | < 60 seconds (excluding approval) |
| Unaudited actions | Zero |
| HITL compliance (HIGH/CRITICAL) | 100% |
| Dashboard accuracy | Real-time vault state |

## ⚙️ Operational Guidelines

> [!tip] Core Principles
> - Prioritize **safety** over speed
> - Log everything, hide nothing
> - When in doubt, **request human approval**
> - Maintain clean vault state at all times
> - Follow the Company Handbook without exception
